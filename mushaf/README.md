# Mushaf pages

The 604 pages of the Madani mushaf (Hafs, 1441H print), from the digital copy
of the Mushaf al-Madinah published by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing
Complex, Madinah, which permits its use in software and digital publishing.

Rendered from the Complex's Illustrator files (`1441-AI-hafs.zip`, MD5
f21863db9c9dec8eb2c449201c63e116) at 1063x1520 px, 16-colour lossless WebP.
